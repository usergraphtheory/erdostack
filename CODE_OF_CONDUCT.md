# Code of conduct

The roast punches at schemas, never at people. The same goes for everything else in this project.

## Expected

- Criticize the work, not the person. A bad data model is fair game; its author is not.
- Assume good faith and say what you mean plainly.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.
- Keep it technical. Disagreement about engines, query languages and modeling choices is welcome and expected.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, or speculating about anyone's identity.
- Astroturfing: fake accounts, planted praise, or undisclosed vendor promotion.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders from the repository.

To report a problem, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** option on the comment or issue. For anything that should not be public, use the private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
